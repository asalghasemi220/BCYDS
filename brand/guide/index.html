Our brand

Who we are, in words everyone on the team can repeat.

Purpose

Helping B.C. kids thrive.

What we do

Consulting and support for kids, families and schools, including students with ADHD, autism and other needs. Hands-on robotics and STEAM help kids focus, create and work together.

Who we serve

  • Families and caregivers
  • Schools, teachers and support staff
  • Partners, funders and the community

What kids gain

  • Discover new interests
  • Solve problems
  • Work together

One-line pitch

BC Youth Discovery Society helps B.C. kids thrive through consulting, support and hands-on robotics and STEAM.

Boilerplate (for bios, grant forms, press)

BC Youth Discovery Society is a British Columbia not-for-profit that helps kids thrive. We work with families and schools to understand each child’s needs and find the right support, including for students with ADHD, autism and other needs. Hands-on robotics and STEAM give kids new ways to focus, create and work together. Program fees cover delivery costs; any surplus supports future youth programs.

Logo

A paper plane taking off with a looping trail: ideas and kids taking flight.

Primary badge. First choice, on white or ice.

Reversed badge. On navy only.

Horizontal. Website headers and narrow spaces.

Horizontal reversed. On navy only.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo equal to x, where x is one quarter of the badge’s width. Nothing (text, edges, other logos) goes inside it.

x

Minimum size

Below these sizes the wordmark becomes hard to read. Use the horizontal logo when space is narrow.

  • Badge: 72 px wide on screen, 20 mm in print
  • Horizontal: 140 px wide on screen, 35 mm in print
  • Favicon and small avatars: use Pilot’s head, never a shrunken logo
04

Logo misuse

The logo only works if it always looks the same.

✕

Don’t change the colours.

✕

Don’t stretch or squash it.

✕

Don’t rotate it.

✕

Don’t add shadows, glows or effects.

✕

Don’t place it on busy photos.

✕

Don’t use the plane on its own. Alone, it reads like a messaging-app icon.

07

Meet Pilot

Our mascot and the friendly face of the society.

Pilot is a curious little robot who helps kids find their way. Pilot’s curly antenna follows the trail in our logo, the goggles are for flying with big ideas, and the heart shows that care comes first.

Wave. Welcomes, hellos, the website.

Cheer. Celebrations, thank-yous, news.

Head. Avatars, favicons, stickers.

On navy, Pilot always sits on an ice circle.

Pilot’s personality

  • Kind, curious and patient
  • Cheers for effort, not perfection
  • Speaks in short, friendly sentences: “Let’s build it together!”

Rules

  • Use the supplied files. Don’t redraw, recolour or distort Pilot.
  • Pilot never gives advice about a specific child.
  • Pilot never endorses products or partners’ services.
  • New poses go through the brand owner first.
08

Icons and shapes

Simple, rounded and calm, in the style of app icons.

Icons

Line icons, 2 px stroke, round ends, white on a navy or blue rounded square. Corner radius is about 30% of the square.

Rounded blocks

Cards and photos use generous rounded corners (20–28 px on screen). Flat colour, no heavy shadows.

STEAM tiles

Big letters on navy, blue and sky tiles. Use for anything about Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts and Math.

Photography and privacy

Real moments, real builds, and every child kept safe.

✓

Close-ups of what kids build.

✓

Hands and projects in a real classroom.

✓

Playful moments where faces aren’t the focus (with consent).

Do

  • Natural light, eye level, real activities
  • Hands, builds and the backs of heads
  • Remove location data from files before sharing
  • Keep a consent record for every identifiable person

Never

  • Show an identifiable child without written parental consent for that use
  • Name a child, their school, class or location
  • Use stock photos as if they were our programs
  • Use partners’ photos without their approval
10

Voice and tone

We sound like a kind, clear teacher who has time for you.

Warm

Talk to parents and teachers as partners. Use “we” and “you”.

Clear

Short sentences, plain words. One idea per sentence.

Encouraging

Celebrate effort and progress, not perfection.

Say it like this

We fix kids’ behaviour problems.
We help kids, families and schools find the right support.
ADHD kids and autistic kids
Students with ADHD, autism and other needs (or the words a family prefers)
Our psychologists diagnose and treat your child.
We start with a conversation to understand each child’s needs.
Guaranteed results in 8 weeks!
Every child learns at their own pace.

Careful words

“Psychologist” is a protected title in B.C., and therapy, counselling, assessment and diagnosis need registered professionals. Use these words only when a registered professional delivers the service. Otherwise say “consulting” and “support”.
