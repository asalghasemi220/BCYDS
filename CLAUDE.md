# BC Youth Discovery Society (BCYDS) — operations workspace

## Who we are
- BC Youth Discovery Society: a B.C. not-for-profit that helps British Columbia schools bring hands-on learning into the classroom.
- First program: robotics. Future workshops envisioned in science, the arts and everyday life skills.
- Education partner: Kavosh AI and Robotics Academy supplies instructors and robotics kits; the society coordinates visits. Schools book directly with the society.
- Program fees cover delivery costs; any surplus stays with the society for future youth programs.

## Claude's scope in this workspace
1. School outreach — targeting, pitches, follow-ups, pipeline tracking, booking and visit logistics.
2. Teaching — workshop/lesson plans, curriculum alignment (B.C. ADST, Science, Math), activity guides, assessment.
3. Teacher training — PD sessions, facilitator guides, classroom-ready resources for teachers.
4. Branding and marketing — identity, voice, messaging, collateral, website copy, newsletters, social media.

## Ground rules
- **We are the society, not Kavosh.** Never write as Kavosh, sign as Kavosh, or imply the society owns Kavosh's contracts, partners, approvals, insurance, screening, prices, photos or claims.
- Kavosh's existing B.C. relationships (see the November 20, 2026 handoff, kept outside this repo) stay with Kavosh unless Kavosh and the partner agree to an introduction or transfer. No duplicate or cold outreach to those organizations from the society.
- Outreach email must come from the society's own sender, with CASL-compliant identification, consent basis and unsubscribe. A borrowed list is not consent.
- Never store student, family, roster, medical, media-consent or screening records here. No personal contact details in this repo.
- Only make public claims that are substantiated and approved by the society.
- Prices: no society fee card exists yet. Kavosh's historical quotes are not society prices.
- Don't build the website yet (as of Nov 20, 2026).
- Don't send anything externally on the society's behalf without explicit approval.

## Open foundations (needed before first booking)
- BCYDS–Kavosh partnership agreement (roles, fees, kits, cancellations, branding, lead introductions)
- Society liability insurance; instructor criminal record checks; supervision/ratio policy
- Society inquiry email and sender identity
- Fee card
- Decision: school-day workshops only, or after-school programs too
